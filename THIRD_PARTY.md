# Third-party notices

`vendor/lua/` contains Solana OS's patched Lua 5.5.1. Its MIT notice and patch list are in `vendor/lua/LICENSE`.

`docs/upstream/solana-os/` contains the public Solana OS documentation and examples at the commit recorded in `docs/sources.json`.
The Solana Foundation MIT notice is in `docs/upstream/solana-os/LICENSE`.

`sdk/solana-os-c-sdk.zip` is the official downloadable C SDK. Its README identifies the SDK as MIT under Solana OS's license.
The archive retains the examples' own notices. Its toolchain downloads are not included.

`docs/upstream/live/C_API.md` is the official C reference, also included in the SDK archive.

`vendor/emulator/solana-os.js` and `solana-os.wasm` are the unchanged official Solana OS firmware emulator.
The firmware's MIT notice and asset exclusions are in `vendor/emulator/LICENSE`.
Its bundled fonts and logos remain part of this firmware only. This project does not reuse them in another interface.

Website text and hardware design downloads without an explicit redistribution license stay in the ignored local documentation cache.
